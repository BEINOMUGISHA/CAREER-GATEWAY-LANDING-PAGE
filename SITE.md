## 🎯 3. Target Demographics & Skill Tier Matrix

### **Tier 1: Skilled & Professional (Degree & Diploma Holders)**
* **Target Demographics:** Ages 21–45; Bachelor's Degree, Postgrad, Diplomas, Professional Licenses.
* **Experience Level:** Mid-Level (3–7 years) to Senior (8+ years).
* **Target Roles:** Registered Nurses, Clinical Officers, Pharmacists, NGO Program Leads, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Officers, Accountants, Branch Managers.
* **Monthly Income Bracket:** `1,200,000 – 3,500,000+ UGX`.
* **Primary Employers:** Private & Regional Hospitals, International NGOs, Financial Institutions, Tertiary Institutions.

---

### **Tier 2: Semi-Skilled & Graduate Entry (High-Demand Operational)**
* **Target Demographics:** Ages 19–38; Certificates, UACE, Driving Permits (Class B/C/E), DIT/UBTEB Trade Certifications, Fresh Graduates.
* **Experience Level:** Entry to Operational (0–3 years).
* **Target Roles:** Heavy Logistics Drivers, Front Desk Officers, Receptionists, Hotel Chefs, Cashiers, Security Guards, Solar & Electrical Technicians, Field Sales Executives.
* **Monthly Income Bracket:** `450,000 – 950,000 UGX` *(+ performance commissions for sales)*.
* **Primary Employers:** Hotels, Safari Lodges, FMCG Distributors, Telco Outlets, Supermarket Chains, Warehouses.

---

### **Tier 3: Non-Skilled & General Labor (Bulk Workforce)**
* **Target Demographics:** Ages 18–40; National ID, verifiable character references, physical fitness (no academic papers required).
* **Experience Level:** Entry-level / Trained on-site.
* **Target Roles:** Cleaners, Farm Hands, Warehouse Loaders, Porters, Kitchen Stewards, Factory Packers, Site Helpers.
* **Monthly Income Bracket:** `250,000 – 500,000 UGX`.
* **Primary Employers:** Commercial Farms, Processing Mills, Logistics Depots, Estates, Cleaning Companies.

## 🚀 6. Free Hosting & Deployment Options

### **Option A: GitHub Pages (Instant)**
1. Go to **Settings > Pages** in your GitHub repository.
2. Select Source: `Deploy from a branch` -> `main` / `(root)`.
3. Live URL: `https://beinomugisha.github.io/CAREER-GATEWAY-LANDING-PAGE/`.

### **Option B: Cloudflare Pages (Recommended - 100% Free, Global Edge CDN & Free SSL)**
1. Sign in to **[dash.cloudflare.com](https://dash.cloudflare.com/)**.
2. Navigate to **Workers & Pages > Create application > Pages > Connect to Git**.
3. Select `BEINOMUGISHA/CAREER-GATEWAY-LANDING-PAGE`.
4. Framework preset: `None` | Build command: *(leave blank)* | Output directory: `/`.
5. Click **Save and Deploy**.

### **Option C: Vercel**
1. Sign in to **[vercel.com](https://vercel.com/)**.
2. Click **Add New > Project > Import `CAREER-GATEWAY-LANDING-PAGE`**.
3. Click **Deploy**.
