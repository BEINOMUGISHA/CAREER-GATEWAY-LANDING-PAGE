🏢 Employers & Hiring Companies

We save you from reviewing 200 unsuitable CVs through a rigorous 4-step pre-screening process:

  • Credential & Identity Verification: We check academic qualifications, professional licenses (e.g. Uganda Nurses & Midwives Council), and National IDs.
  • Reference & Background Checks: We contact previous employers and referees to confirm work ethics, punctuality, and conduct.
  • Preliminary Competency Interview: We test domain aptitude and alignment with your salary budget.
  • Shortlist Delivery: You only receive the top 2 to 3 pre-screened finalists ready for immediate interviews.

For urgent roles (sales reps, hotel front desk, drivers, warehouse crews), we provide qualified candidate profiles within 48 to 72 hours. For specialized medical or administrative posts, shortlists are delivered within 3 to 5 business days.

We provide a 30-day replacement guarantee. If a candidate leaves voluntarily or fails to meet the agreed job standards during the first month, Career Gateway Agency will provide a pre-screened replacement candidate at no extra recruitment cost.

💰 Skill Tiers & Salary Expectations

Based on current market benchmarks across Mbarara, Kabale, Fort Portal, and Hoima:

  • Non-Skilled & General Labor (Cleaners, Porters, Farm Hands): 250,000 – 450,000 UGX / month.
  • Semi-Skilled & Operational (Drivers, Receptionists, Cashiers, Chefs): 450,000 – 950,000 UGX / month.
  • Graduate & Entry Level (Trainees, Telco/FMCG Sales Reps): 500,000 – 1,000,000 UGX (+ commissions).
  • Skilled & Professional (Nurses, Program Officers, Accountants): 1,200,000 – 3,500,000+ UGX / month.

Our core active job seeker demographic is between 18 and 45 years of age, encompassing fresh school leavers, diploma holders, university graduates, skilled trade practitioners, and mid-to-senior level career managers.
